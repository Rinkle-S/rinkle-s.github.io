About me

Product, data, and a few good detours.

I'm a product manager who likes the messy middle: the bit between a vague user problem and a feature that's live and getting used.

My story

I started in engineering and data science at IIT Madras, where an integrated master's in Data Science taught me to let evidence lead the decision. That instinct carried into product: today I'm a Client Engagement Manager at Corridor Platforms, a decision-intelligence fintech for banks.

I own Tier-1 U.S. bank engagements end to end, coordinating releases across engineering, QA, and design, scoping PRDs, running UAT, and shipping features that users adopt. I care about closing feedback loops and improving iteratively, which is how my team lifted NPS from 6 to 8 and put a model-monitoring tool into production that now tracks 380+ models.

Before Corridor, I worked across strategy and product at BCG and an agri-fintech startup, GreyMatter, which taught me to be comfortable with ambiguity, sharp prioritisation, and translating data into decisions leaders trust.
